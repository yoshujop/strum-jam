    Claude (optional)

    Song lookup works without a key. Songs that aren't in the chord datasets, and Story mode, use Claude: through Strum Jam's shared lookup service (a few free lookups a day; every answer is saved for all players), or with your own Anthropic API key, which skips those limits. The key stays in this browser only.

    With your own key, Claude reads chord sources on the web only for songs that aren't in the chord datasets, or whose dataset chart doesn't match the recording (web search must be allowed for your organization in the Claude Console: Settings → Privacy → Web search).
